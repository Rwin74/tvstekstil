DENİZLİ, TÜRKİYE / TVS TEXTILE

We are TVS Textile.
We make our textiles in Denizli.

We founded TVS Textile in 2021. We manufacture in our own factory at Akçeşme M. 2605 Sk. No: 40 in Merkezefendi, Denizli. Our product range brings together home textiles, hotel and spa textiles, and baby collections.

We want our first conversation to be useful. Tell us who the product is for, which sizes and materials you have in mind and where the order is intended to go. Where a specification or document needs confirmation, we prefer to make that question explicit rather than present an assumption as a finished product fact.
