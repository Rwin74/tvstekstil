How we approach this product

We put the final specification first

When you enquire about a baby sleeping bag, we ask for the intended age and size range, the season of use and your preferred fastening arrangement. We want to understand the construction you are considering rather than choose a thermal rating from a product photograph.

Thermal performance, fit, labelling and applicable safety testing need to be verified for the final design and destination market. We do not publish an assumed TOG value or certification. Your enquiry should identify the requirements you need to meet so that the conversation can focus on documented specifications and the next steps for that product.

Before you request a quotation

Can we assume a thermal rating from the sleeping-bag image?

No. Our concept image does not establish a thermal rating, fabric composition or safety-test result. If your range requires a particular rating, include it in the brief together with the intended sizing and fastening. The evidence and applicable requirements must be confirmed for the finished sleeping bag.

How do we prepare a wholesale enquiry?

Send us the quantity for each size or colour, the destination country and your preferred schedule. Include your reference images and the specifications below. We will discuss the outstanding details with you; minimum quantities, sample availability, prices and lead times need confirmation for your particular project.
