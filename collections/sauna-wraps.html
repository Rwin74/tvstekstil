Sauna wraps

We see a sauna wrap as a product whose fit deserves as much attention as its fabric.

Enquire about this product ↗
How we approach this product

We work from coverage and fastening

Before discussing a sauna wrap, we want to know the coverage you have in mind. Length, waist range and fastening preferences determine how the product should be specified. We ask you to describe the intended spa or sauna setting rather than assume that one size will suit every guest.

Fabric weight and absorbency requirements come next, together with laundering conditions. We also want to clarify whether you need one unisex format or separate variants. Breaking the enquiry down this way gives the quotation a clearer basis and helps avoid an attractive photograph standing in for the actual measurements.
