How we approach this product

The feel we want to define together

When you describe a muslin duvet cover to us, we want to understand how it should feel on the bed. A light, airy appearance and a more substantial layered fabric lead to different briefs. We therefore start the conversation with composition, the number of gauze layers and your preferred finished dimensions.

We also want to discuss what happens after washing. The agreed dimensions should make sense alongside the expected shrinkage and care instructions. If this cover belongs to a larger bedding range, send us the pillowcase sizes and colour references too; we can then discuss the collection as a whole.

Before you request a quotation

How do we specify a muslin duvet cover collection?

We ask you to send a texture reference as well as the dimensions. Tell us which gauze construction and composition you want to discuss, whether pillowcases are included and how the finished set should be packaged. We agree the fabric and care expectations for the actual product before treating the brief as complete.

How do we prepare a wholesale enquiry?

Send us the quantity for each size or colour, the destination country and your preferred schedule. Include your reference images and the specifications below. We will discuss the outstanding details with you; minimum quantities, sample availability, prices and lead times need confirmation for your particular project.
