Baby swaddles

We see a baby swaddle brief as a conversation about fabric, dimensions and the product’s intended use.

Enquire about this product ↗
How we approach this product

We ask you to describe the purpose clearly

The word swaddle can cover different textile formats. We therefore want to agree what the product is intended to be, along with the finished dimensions and composition. Colour and print references are helpful once that foundation is clear. If several sizes are planned, list them separately with their quantities.

We also want to discuss the instructions and labelling required for your market. We do not make sleep-safety claims from a fabric description. The final use, care information and any applicable requirements need to be considered alongside the actual product specification before an order is agreed.
